Contest Rules & Prize Terms

Last Updated: May 13, 2026

NO PURCHASE NECESSARY. Global Goal 2026 is free to use. Points can only be earned through engagement activities described on the Platform. Void where prohibited by law.

1. Sponsor

Global Goal 2026 ("Sponsor") operates this independent fan engagement platform. This is NOT affiliated with FIFA or any official tournament organizer.

2. Eligibility

  • Open to individuals aged 18 or older worldwide
  • Employees of Sponsor and immediate family members are not eligible
  • Void where prohibited by local law
  • One account per person. Multiple accounts will be disqualified

3. How to Participate

  • Create a free account on globalgoal2026.com
  • Complete tasks to earn points as described on the Platform
  • Accumulate points to redeem prizes or rank on leaderboards
  • All tasks must be completed honestly and in good faith

4. Points System

  • Daily Check-in: 20 points (resets every 24 hours)
  • Active Time (20+ min): 30 points per session
  • Watch Video: 50 points per video (must watch 80%+)
  • Refer Friend: 50 points when they complete first task
  • YouTube Subscribe: 100 points (one-time, verified)
  • Newsletter Subscribe: 50 points (one-time)
  • Comment: 40 points per approved comment

5. Prize Redemption

  • Prizes are available while supplies last
  • Points must be sufficient for the selected prize
  • Shipping address must be valid and complete
  • Allow 7-21 business days for delivery
  • International shipping may incur customs fees (recipient's responsibility)
  • Prizes are non-transferable and cannot be exchanged for cash

6. Anti-Fraud Policy

We actively monitor for fraudulent activity including:

  • Bot usage or automated scripts
  • Multiple accounts per individual
  • Fake referrals or self-referrals
  • VPN abuse to circumvent restrictions
  • Exploitation of bugs or glitches

Violations will result in immediate account termination, point forfeiture, and prize disqualification. We reserve the right to pursue legal action for fraud.

7. Taxes

Winners are responsible for any taxes associated with prize winnings. Prizes valued over $600 (USD) may require tax form completion.

8. Disputes

All disputes will be resolved at Sponsor's discretion. Decisions are final. Sponsor reserves the right to modify or cancel the program at any time.

9. Contact

For contest questions: contests@globalgoal2026.com
