About Global Goal 2026

An independent fan community built by football lovers, for football lovers.

Our Mission

Global Goal 2026 was created to bring football fans together during the biggest summer of soccer in North American history. We're an independent, fan-operated platform that rewards engagement and celebrates the beautiful game.

We believe that being a fan should be fun, rewarding, and social. That's why we built a points system that lets you earn while you enjoy the content you already love.

What We Offer

  • Curated match highlights and football content
  • A rewarding points system for engagement
  • Real prizes from PlayStation 5 to gift cards
  • Live leaderboards to compete with fans worldwide
  • Community discussions and fan interaction
  • Completely free to join and participate
