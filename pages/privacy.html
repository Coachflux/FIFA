Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 13, 2026

At Global Goal 2026, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Username, email address, password (encrypted)
  • Activity Data: Points earned, tasks completed, videos watched, time spent on site
  • Referral Data: Referral code usage, invited friends
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information, cookies
  • Prize Shipping: Name, shipping address, phone number (only when redeeming prizes)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and improve the Platform
  • To track points and process prize redemptions
  • To prevent fraud and abuse
  • To send newsletters and updates (with your consent)
  • To analyze usage patterns and improve user experience
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Keep you logged in
  • Track your points and task completion
  • Analyze site traffic and usage
  • Serve relevant advertisements

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features may not work properly.

4. Advertising

We display advertisements through Google AdSense and other partners. These services may use cookies to serve personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ad Settings.

5. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Service providers (hosting, analytics, email delivery)
  • Advertising partners (in aggregated, non-identifiable form)
  • Legal authorities when required by law

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures including encryption, secure servers, and regular backups. However, no online system is 100% secure.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction or deletion
  • Opt out of marketing emails
  • Export your data

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@globalgoal2026.com

8. Children's Privacy

Our Platform is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will notify users of significant changes via email or site notice.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact: privacy@globalgoal2026.com
